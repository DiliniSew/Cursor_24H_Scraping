# Tour pages

* **1. Tour Overview**
* **1.1. Package Name:** Sri Lanka & The Maldives Tour
* **1.2. Theme:** Barefoot Luxury
* **1.3. Duration:** 10 Nights (7 Nights in Sri Lanka, 3 Nights in the Maldives)


* **2. Core Inclusions**
* **2.1. Accommodation:** Hotel stay for 10 nights
* **2.2. Meals:** Tailored to customer preferences
* **2.3. Transport:** Private air-conditioned vehicle for transfers and sightseeing


* **3. Itinerary Breakdown (11-Day Journey)**
* **3.1. Phase 1: Sri Lanka Exploration (Days 1–7)**
* **Day 1: Arrival & Coastal Rest** – Airport to Negombo
* **Day 2: Culture & Wildlife Introduction** – Negombo to Sigiriya (via Elephant Care Relief Foundation & Sigiriya Rock Fortress)
* **Day 3: Ancient Ruins & Safari** – Sigiriya to Polonnaruwa (Walking tour of ruins, safari at Minneriya/Kaudulla National Park)
* **Day 4: Hill Capital Transition** – Sigiriya to Kandy (via Matale Spice Garden & historical temple shrines)
* **Day 5: Kandy Immersive City Tour** – Temple of the Tooth Relic, Peradeniya Botanical Gardens, Udawattekele Forest Reserve, and a Cultural Dance Performance
* **Day 6: Upcountry Scenic Mountains** – Kandy to Nuwara Eliya via a 3.5-hour train ride (Tea factory tour & colonial city walk)
* **Day 7: Drive to the Commercial Capital** – Nuwara Eliya to Colombo (Pettah market, Fort area, and city exploration)


* **3.2. Phase 2: Maldives Relaxation (Days 8–10)**
* **Day 8: Island Fly-out** – Colombo to Male via flight (Speedboat/seaplane transfer to beach resort, optional night fishing)
* **Day 9: Marine Life Excursion** – Pods of dolphins up close & Manta Ray watching
* **Day 10: Undersea Adventure** – Scuba diving, snorkeling, and swimming with turtles


* **3.3. Phase 3: Departure (Day 11)**
* **Day 11: Final Transfer** – Resort to Male Airport for flight home




* **4. Featured Experiences & Highlights**
* **4.1. Cultural Heritage:** Ancient Ceylon ruins, Sigiriya, and traditional Kandyan dance shows
* **4.2. Ocean & Wildlife Adjacencies:** Whale and dolphin watching, coral reef snorkeling, scuba diving, and elephant interaction
